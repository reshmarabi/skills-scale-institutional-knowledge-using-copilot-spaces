# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This README serves as the central entry point for OctoAcme project management documentation. It gives contributors and stakeholders a quick overview of the project management processes used by the team and links to the detailed guidance for each stage of the delivery lifecycle.

## Project Management Processes Overview

OctoAcme follows a structured project lifecycle that keeps work aligned to business outcomes, transparent for stakeholders, and adaptable as priorities shift.

- Initiation: Define the project objective, stakeholders, success criteria, and initial scope. Capture the business need, expected outcomes, and approvals required before work starts.
- Planning: Create a project plan with milestones, timelines, responsibilities, and dependencies. Establish a risk register, communication cadence, and the key artifacts needed to execute the work.
- Execution & Tracking: Run work in planned increments, monitor progress against milestones, and keep task status visible through boards, check-ins, and status updates.
- Risks & Communication: Identify and log risks, set escalation paths, and maintain regular stakeholder communication so issues are addressed early and decisions are documented.
- Release & Deployment: Prepare release checklists, confirm readiness, and follow deployment runbooks to minimize disruption and support a smooth rollout.
- Retrospective & Continuous Improvement: Review what went well, capture lessons learned, and convert feedback into actionable improvement items for future delivery cycles.
- Roles & Personas: Clarify responsibilities across product, project, engineering, quality, and leadership roles to ensure accountability and decision ownership.
